# Streamer Mode

Open **Mod Settings → Streamer Mode** to choose which personal information appears while you stream. The master switch starts off. **Hide my IDs, name, and alliance** starts selected. **Hide chat sender names** and **Compact player ship labels** are opt-in. Turning the master switch off restores your usual presentation and keeps your choices for next time.

## Coverage

| Control | Protected presentation | Additional exposure to check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hide my IDs, name, and alliance | General Settings shows **Hidden** for the Player ID and covers the identifier next to **Signed into Scopely as**. Player ID **Copy** uses the real ID and **Switch Account** stays available. The separate native Player ID label is covered when its display hook is available. In your own Player Profile, your name, alliance name, tag, and emblem are covered. Inspected profiles of other players display normally. | Profile titles, avatars, stats, and account details opened through other apps. |
| Hide chat sender names | Sender names in HUD previews, full and side chat, direct chat and channel lists, and blocked-user rows. Messages stay readable. Use the game's chat controls to close or open the panel. | Message text, titles, avatars, attachments, and other popups can still identify a sender. |
| Compact player ship labels | Player ships on the System map use the Compact layout. This layout keeps the ship icon and level and omits commander names and alliance tags. Your usual player label detail returns when you turn this option or Streamer Mode off. | Selected ship cards and inspected profiles can show details about other players. |

The menu shows **Protection unavailable** when the required hooks or frame delivery are missing. **On · Check protections** means a selected protection is unavailable or an active mask could not be verified. You can still turn an installed protection off. The masks act on game presentation. The game still has the original values for account features, navigation, and messaging.

## Validation

Check General Settings, your own Player Profile, an inspected profile from another player, and chat with Streamer Mode saved on. With **Compact player ship labels** selected, enter a system and zoom in on a player ship. Check its label for the icon and level and confirm that the commander name and alliance tag are absent. Repeat after switching systems. Turn Streamer Mode off and verify that your profile fields, account values, and usual player label detail return. Verify the Player ID Copy action and readable chat. Use synthetic identifiers where possible and record pass/fail results without capturing IDs or chat contents. Record rendered checks against the exact client and mod build tested.
